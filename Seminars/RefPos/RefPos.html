Reflection Positivity and Invertible Phases

Learning seminar, Topology group, Utrecht University

About

In this learning seminar we will work through Freed and Hopkins' paper Reflection positivity and invertible topological phases, together with Freed's CBMS lecture notes Lectures on Field Theory and Topology, with an emphasis on the homotopy theory. Starting from bordism and Thom spectra, we will see how the cobordism hypothesis and the Galatius–Madsen–Tillmann–Weiss theorem turn invertible field theories into maps of spectra, how Anderson duality enters, and how reflection positivity is encoded by C2-equivariant stable homotopy theory. We will also discuss the higher spin-statistics theorem, and end with Adams spectral sequence computations recovering the ten-fold way. The physics will be kept to the motivation needed to state the results.
