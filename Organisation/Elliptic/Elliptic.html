Elliptic Cohomology & Friends

Utrecht University, April 19–23, 2027

About

This is a one-week conference focused on elliptic cohomology and its interactions with other areas of mathematics and physics. It features introductory lectures on equivariant elliptic cohomology by Jack Davies, on conformal field theories by Lukas Woike, and on the Segal–Stolz–Teichner program by Mayuko Yamashita.

Many researchers work in areas related to elliptic cohomology, and we hope to bring them together for fruitful collaboration and conversation.

Getting There

By plane: The nearest airport is Amsterdam Schiphol (AMS). Direct trains run from Schiphol Airport to Utrecht Centraal several times an hour; the journey takes about 30 minutes.

By train: Utrecht Centraal is a major hub of the Dutch railway network, with direct connections to Amsterdam, Rotterdam, The Hague and Eindhoven, as well as international trains from Germany.

From Utrecht Centraal to Utrecht Science Park: Trams and buses run regularly from Utrecht Centraal to Utrecht Science Park (about 15–20 minutes).

Tickets: On trains, trams and buses you can check in and out with a contactless debit or credit card (or phone); remember to check out at the end of your journey.
